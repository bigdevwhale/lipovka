# Lipovka — the world bible

«Почта в Липовке» / "Lipovka Post" is a pedometer. The player is the new postman of Lipovka, a small
Russian village by the river Lipka. Real steps carry the bag: every morning letters arrive at the post
office, and the player's walking delivers them house by house. The postman never reads other people's
letters: at the gate the resident opens the envelope, shares the news, sometimes gives a small gift
and sometimes hands a reply to carry. Story by story, the lives of the village unfold over years.

## Tone

Warm, quiet and lived-in. Small concrete details (the smell of a stove, a goat called Zorka, a chipped
blue cup) rather than big events. Gentle humour. Some bittersweet moments, but no tragedies: nobody
dies on the page, no illness drama, no war, no politics, no religion. People are kind but not saccharine:
they grumble, are shy, make mistakes, reconcile. Time is slow: seasons, harvests, the river freezing
and breaking up, children growing.

Residents address the postman with polite «вы» (plural verbs: «вы принесли», «вы промокли») so the
text works for any gender. In English, "you". The postman's name is never needed.

Present day, a timeless Russian countryside: landline phones are rare, there is a mobile signal "on the
hill by the church ruins… no, on the hill by the water tower", but people write letters because they like
to, because the elderly do, because parcels and postcards need the post, because it's Lipovka.

## Places (districts) — unlocked by the total distance the player has walked

| id | district | unlocks at | what is there |
|----|----------|-----------|----------------|
| d0 | Липовка / Lipovka | start | the post office, the shop, the club with the library, the school, the watermill, the apiary, the medical post (ФАП) |
| d1 | Заречье / Zarechye ("Across the River") | 40 km (the bridge is repaired) | an old farmstead (хутор), the forester's lodge, a woodcarver's workshop, twin sisters with goats |
| d2 | Разъезд 118 км / Halt 118 km (the station) | 150 km | a tiny railway halt with a telegraph, the local train "Kukushka" |
| d3 | Озёрки / Ozyorki | 350 km | a fishing hamlet on a big lake, a boat shed, a painter's cottage |
| d4 | Сосновый кордон / Pine Cordon | 700 km | a weather station deep in the forest, a large forest family |
| d5 | Городок / Gorodok (the district town) | 1200 km | the newspaper office, the local history museum |

An average walker (6,000 steps a day) walks about 1,600 km a year, so the last district opens in the
second year. Arcs of a district start when it opens.

## Residents

Ids in brackets are used in the content files. Ages are at the start.

### Lipovka (d0)
- **Анна Ветрова / Anna Vetrova** [anna], 27. Works at the dairy farm, sews beautifully. Waits for letters
  from her fiancé **Степан / Stepan**, a sailor on the motor ship «Онега» (Northern Shipping Company).
  Arc: Stepan comes home by Pokrov (mid-October) → they marry next summer → build a porch and a
  bathhouse → Anna opens a little sewing workshop → a baby (in the third year). Stepan becomes the
  skipper of the river ferry. Anna's house has a rowan tree and a red roof.
- **Фёдор Ильич Жуков / Fyodor Ilyich Zhukov** [fedor], 64, the miller. Widower. Keeps the old watermill
  turning. Arc: the mill wheel needs a new oak shaft → he corresponds with an old millwrights' society →
  his grandson **Петя / Petya** (10, from Vologda) spends the summers → the mill starts baking bread on
  Saturdays → a late, shy friendship (and then more) with Agafya Petrovna.
- **Агафья Петровна Сорокина / Agafya Petrovna Sorokina** [agafya], 78, widow, knits for everyone.
  At first she receives only bills and the pension. Arc: a letter to her sister **Клавдия / Klavdia** comes
  back "addressee moved away" → the postman's patience and the town archive help find Klavdia (in
  Petrozavodsk) → letters, then a visit the next summer → Agafya starts a knitting circle at the club →
  she and Fyodor Ilyich.
- **Николай Семёнович Лебедев / Nikolai Semyonovich Lebedev** [teacher], 71, retired village teacher
  (taught 40 years, physics and astronomy). Former pupils write to him from all over: **Илья / Ilya** is
  now an astronomer at an observatory in the Caucasus. Arc: pupils' letters → he writes a memoir
  "Сорок выпусков" → the school's jubilee reunion → a comet he and Varya watch through Ilya's telescope →
  in the third year he moves to his daughter in Gorodok, but keeps writing (to the postman too).
- **Кузнецовы / the Kuznetsovs**: **Варя / Varya** [varya], 7, first grader, curious, writes to Ded Moroz
  every December and gets answers from Veliky Ustyug; a pen-pal in Kamchatka. **Ольга / Olga** [olga], 34,
  her mother, the feldsher at the medical post: medicine parcels, studies by correspondence to become a
  doctor (diploma in the third year). **Сергей / Sergey** [sergey], 36, tractor driver, fixes anything,
  writes to his brother in Murmansk. Varya grows up: 7 → 8 → 9 → 10.
- **Пётр Михайлович Рой / Pyotr Mikhailovich Roy, "Mikhalych"** [mikhalych], 66, beekeeper. Sends honey
  all over the country. Arc: a beekeepers' club newsletter → a live queen bee arrives by post (!) → a medal
  at the regional honey fair → Varya and later Kolya help at the apiary → he writes a little book about bees.
- **Зинаида Павловна Клюева / Zinaida Pavlovna Klyueva, "Zina"** [zina], 52, runs the village shop and
  knows all the news. Catalogues, invoices, her son **Артём / Artyom** studies in St Petersburg. Arc: she
  dreams of opening a tea room ("Чайная") in the back of the shop → does it → Artyom brings his fiancée.
- **Коля Рябинин / Kolya Ryabinin** [kolya], 15, radio amateur, lives with his grandmother **Нина / Nina**.
  QSL cards come from all over the world (rare stamps!). Arc: first contact → a contact with Antarctica →
  a contact with **Глеб / Gleb** at the Pine Cordon weather station (d4) → exams → technical college in
  Gorodok in the third year, writes home.
- **Лидия Аркадьевна Белова / Lidia Arkadyevna Belova** [lidia], 45, runs the club and the library. Book
  parcels, posters, film nights, an amateur theatre. Arc: the library is threatened with closure → the
  village writes letters → it is saved → the theatre stages "Morozko" at New Year.
- **Соколовы / the Sokolovs**: **Марина / Marina** [marina], 31, illustrator, and **Дима / Dima** [dima], 33,
  programmer, moved from Moscow to live in the village. Arc: city people learning the village (their first
  winter, the stove smokes, a goat, a vegetable garden that fails then succeeds) → they stay → a baby in
  the second year → Marina illustrates a children's book about Lipovka.

### Zarechye (d1)
- **Ерофей Кузьмич Северов / Yerofei Kuzmich Severov** [erofey], 89, lives alone on the farmstead with
  the goat **Зорька / Zorka**. In his youth a radio operator at polar stations. Letters from his old
  expedition comrades and great-grandchildren. Arc: his 90th birthday, when the whole village crosses the
  bridge; a museum asks for his polar diaries.
- **Громовы / the Gromovs**: **Андрей / Andrei** [andrei], 40, the forester; **Таня / Tanya** [tanya], 38,
  nurses wild animals (an owlet, an elk calf, a fox with a bad paw). Letters from the nature reserve.
- **Григорий Резников, Гриша / Grigory "Grisha" Reznikov** [grisha], 35, woodcarver: carved window
  frames (наличники), spoons, toys. Orders by post, an exhibition in Moscow. He carves a new sign for the
  post office.
- **Сёстры Ласточкины / the Lastochkin sisters**: twins **Вера / Vera** [vera] and **Надя / Nadya** [nadya],
  72, goats and cheese, they argue about everything and are inseparable; a cheese competition.

### Halt 118 km (d2)
- **Тамара Ивановна Гудкова / Tamara Ivanovna Gudkova** [tamara], 58, telegraph operator and keeper
  of the halt. Sends telegrams. Arc: retirement → trains her successor **Лёша Мухин / Lyosha Mukhin**
  [lyosha], 19 → a late romance with the train driver Viktor.
- **Виктор Павлович Стрельцов / Viktor Pavlovich Streltsov** [viktor], 50, drives the local train
  "Kukushka" (Cuckoo), widower, collects railway lanterns.

### Ozyorki (d3)
- **Матвей Савельич Окунев / Matvei Savelyich Okunev** [matvei], 74, fisherman and boatbuilder.
  His son **Илья / Ilya** (not the astronomer) works in the Arctic. Builds a wooden boat over a winter.
- **Ирина Светлова / Irina Svetlova** [irina], 30, a painter who came for the light on the lake. Paints
  postcards and gives them away. An exhibition; falls in love with Matvei's son when he returns.

### Pine Cordon (d4)
- **Глеб Андреевич Туманов / Gleb Andreyevich Tumanov** [gleb], 45, the meteorologist alone at the weather
  station. Radio contact with Kolya. Lonely at first; letters with the observatory; adopts a dog.
- **Белкины / the Belkins**: **Дарья / Darya** [darya], 39, and **Тимофей / Timofei** [timofei], 42, five
  children taught partly by correspondence. Lots of letters: school assignments, drawings, a lost tooth.

### Gorodok (d5)
- **Павел Сергеевич Редькин / Pavel Sergeyevich Redkin** [pavel], 55, editor of the district newspaper
  «Сельская новь» / "Village News". Wants a column "Notes of a Postman" (from the player).
- **Софья Марковна Вайс / Sofia Markovna Weiss** [sofia], 67, curator of the local history museum,
  collects the history of the post: old postmen, letters, stamps.

### The postman [me]
The player. Letters addressed to "me" appear on the post office desk in the morning and need no
walking. They carry the seasonal mysteries, the pen-pal postwoman from another village, the district post
office's milestone letters, the newspaper column, the history of the previous postman.

## Animals and fixed details
- **Сургуч / Surguch** ("Sealing Wax"), the ginger post-office cat, sleeps on the letters.
- **Бублик / Bublik** ("Bagel"), a stray tan dog with a curled tail, starts walking with the postman after
  the first week of walks and stays.
- The post office: a blue roof, ходики (a wall clock with a cat face), a green enamel kettle with white
  dots, pigeonholes, a postal scale. The postman lives in the room upstairs.
- The district post office is in Gorodok («Районный узел связи»).
- The newspaper is «Сельская новь» (weekly, Thursdays).
